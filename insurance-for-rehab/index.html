Insurance for Rehab in Indiana

Insurance coverage for rehab depends on the plan, provider network, diagnosis, medical necessity, authorization, level of care, and services delivered. A benefits check is not a guarantee of payment, so request both insurer confirmation and a provider estimate.

Common questions

Insurance for Rehab in Indiana FAQs

Does accepting insurance mean in network?

No. A provider may bill a plan without having an in-network contract.

What information should I get from the insurer?

Record representative, date, reference number, network status, benefits, deductible, coinsurance, authorization, and exclusions.

Can rehab be denied as not medically necessary?

Coverage decisions can depend on plan criteria and clinical documentation. Ask about review and appeal steps.

Does Medicare cover substance-use treatment?

Medicare covers certain mental health and substance-use services when requirements are met; confirm the provider and service.
